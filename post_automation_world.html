What to watch simple reading
Workers start at homes, commute to routine factories or offices, then consume and save. Robots and AI capacity grow over time. The crimson stream is AI rent leaving the country unless the AI value levy intercepts it.
Field guide entities
Households

Green and blue people earn wages, consume at the market, save wealth, and can become unemployed as automation rises.

Robots and factories

Orange squares and factory districts represent physical automation. Robot taxes collect from this local hardware income.

AI rent and foreign owner

The red stream is model/IP rent. If untaxed, it flows offshore and raises foreign ownership of the local capital stock.

Government

Teal flows are tax receipts. The public fund rises when taxes exceed transfers, and falls when benefits outrun the base.
