Simulation concept

Post-automation economy as a stock-flow machine

The model treats automation as a shift in production from labour income toward capital income and foreign-held AI intellectual-property rent. Each period updates production, household wealth, ownership, taxes, government balances, and foreign claims while checking that financial stocks still add up.

N Heterogeneous households with persistent returns to wealth.
K Capital stock split between robots and AI compute.
R Mobile AI rent that can leave as a licence fee.
Balance sheet actors
H

Combines labour, robots, and compute; issues claims on capital stock.

Government

Collects policy revenue, pays UBI or benefits, may build a sovereign equity fund.

Rest of world

Owns foreign equity and receives the AI rent when IP is booked abroad.

Nested CES top layer combines routine and cognitive value added into output Y.
The split matters.

A robot tax lands on physical, competitive capital income. A digital-services levy or withholding reaches the AI rent because it taxes recognised AI value or the licence fee crossing the border. That distinction is the core reason the policy ranking changes once AI IP is treated as mobile.

Distribution: wealth concentration is generated, not imposed

Income draw

Wages and capital returns arrive

Households receive cluster-specific wages and a share of capital income based on equity ownership.

→
Return heterogeneity

Persistent return types compound

Each household has an AR(1) return type, so rich households can keep earning higher equity returns.

→
Turnover and saving

Demographic resets keep a stationary tail

Some households reset each period, while wage saving provides the inflow that prevents total lock-in.

Measured Gini

Domestic inequality

Computed from domestic household equity. It can fall when capital leaves the household balance sheet.

True Gini

Domestic plus offshore wealth

Attributes fled capital back to its original household owner, separating real redistribution from measurement effects.

Top shares

Tail diagnostics

Top 1 percent and top 10 percent shares show whether concentration is broadly upper-class or apex-heavy.

03

Policy levers hit different bases

Lever Tax base Simulation effect What it can miss
wealth_taxStock tax on domestic household equity Declared household wealth after avoidance Transfers part of domestic equity to the state and can compress measured wealth inequality. Foreign-held IP rent and offshore capital unless explicitly attributed back.
income_tax + ubiFlow tax and transfer Wages plus household capital income Redistributes current income and can fund benefits if the tax base remains broad enough. Long-run wealth concentration when labour share falls.
robot_taxSource levy on robots Physical robotic capital income Easy to collect on assets located inside the host economy. AI model rent booked as a cross-border licence fee.
dst_aiDigital-services-style levy AI value recognised in the host economy Reaches the durable AI surplus and slows foreign accumulation of domestic equity. Profit shifting if recognition can be moved to a low-tax entity.
tax_repatWithholding on rent Licence fee as it crosses the border Can be banked by the state or rebated per capita for stronger equality effects. Treaty limits, avoidance, and any rent not recognised as a cross-border charge.

05

Python script shape

Params

Calibration

A dataclass stores agent count, CES elasticities, automation ramps, saving propensities, taxes, and mobility parameters.

Production

Nested CES

Routine and cognitive clusters compute factor shares, output, competitive returns, and rent peeled from AI value added.

Agents

Household update

Vectorized households receive wages, heterogeneous capital returns, taxes, transfers, consumption, saving, and demographic resets.

Reports

Scenario outputs

The script emits period-level CSVs and terminal summaries for Gini, rent share, revenue, ownership, and accounting gaps.

Build discipline.

The script should be treated as an inspectable simulation lab: every scenario is just a parameter set, every tax base is named, and every run reports accounting gaps so model bugs are visible instead of hidden behind attractive charts.
